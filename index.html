Panggih Kusuma Ningrum, Ph.D.

I hold a PhD in Natural Language Processing (NLP). Currently, I am a Research Associate in NLP at the Centre de Recherches Interdisciplinaires et Transculturelles (CRIT), Université Marie et Louis Pasteur, France. Alongside research, I also design and teach NLP courses at GRETA-CFA de Besançon, helping shape the next generation of data and AI professionals.

In 2026, I obtained national qualification as a Maître de Conférences (MCF) in the French public university system, accredited by the Conseil National des Universités (CNU) in Section 07 – Sciences du langage and Section 27 – Informatique. This qualification confirms my eligibility for tenured‑track faculty positions (equivalent to Associate Professor) in French universities and attests, through the formal evaluation by the national Conseil National des Universités (CNU), to the quality and coherence of my research and teaching profile in natural language processing, computational linguistics, and related fields.

Outside of academic world, you’ll find me composing music with my indie-rock band L'aire de jeu, experimenting in the kitchen, or cruising on my longboard.
